# WP6-FI1-v1 qualification boundary

## Authorized result

The locked campaign reproduces the exact mass branches used with Theorem 3.1
of Morales-Ruiz and Simon (2009).  Subject to the pinned theorem, it supports:

For every finite positive mass triple and every integer `d>=2`, the Newtonian
three-body vector field admits no meromorphic first integral that is
functionally independent of all classical first integrals.  For each mass
triple the proof selects at least one of two homothetic Lagrange witness
curves on which the necessary condition fails.

The project independently checks the symmetric mass invariant, the real
Lagrange Hessian spectrum, both exceptional mass branches, exact Hessian
specializations, and the distinction from the older first-NVE polynomial
invariants.

## What this changes

`WP4-MR-REPL-v1` proved failure of complete meromorphic Liouville
integrability on a particular fixed-nonzero-angular-momentum planar reduced
model.  It explicitly did not exclude one extra integral.

`WP6-FI1-v1` supplies the stronger one-extra-integral conclusion for the
unreduced Newtonian vector field relative to the full classical integral
family.  It does not obtain that result by strengthening the WP4 NVE.

## Exclusions

This qualification does not prove:

- absence of a function defined only on an individual fixed-energy or
  fixed-angular-momentum reduced leaf;
- absence of continuous, smooth, real-analytic but non-meromorphic, or other
  non-meromorphic integrals;
- that one named witness curve works for every mass triple;
- a global orbit classification;
- anything about collision continuation.

The first-NVE polynomial-invariant values `1/3`, `8/27`, and `2/9` are not
actual nonlinear first integrals and are not exceptions to the authorized
2009 theorem.
