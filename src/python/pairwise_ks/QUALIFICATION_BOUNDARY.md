# Pairwise KS Boundary

This package applies the qualified canonical KS cotangent lift independently
to the three oriented relative phase states of a qualified Newtonian
three-body state. Each pair is lifted at `Xi=0`, and the product is constrained
by triangle closure in position and relative velocity. The independent
`S1^3` action is retained as gauge freedom.

Dimension accounting is:

- three KS phase spaces: 24;
- three `Xi=0` restrictions and three `S1` quotients: 18;
- three position-closure and three velocity-closure equations: 12.

The result is a redundant kinematic representation of the 12D
translation-reduced N3BP phase space. It is not three decoupled Kepler
problems, does not qualify a globally canonical embedding of the aggregate
pair variables, and does not define a shared Sundman time or regularized N3BP
Hamiltonian. Binary collision in the input remains outside this v1 lift
because the physical-to-KS section is undefined at collision.
