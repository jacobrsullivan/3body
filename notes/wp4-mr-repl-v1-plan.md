# WP4-MR-REPL-v1 Qualification Plan

Date: 2026-07-23

## Focused Goal

Replace the only externally assumed mathematical premise in
`WP4-LIFT-NI-v2` with a project-owned, exact reproduction. Starting from the
declared translation- and rotation-reduced Newtonian Hamiltonian, derive the
parabolic Lagrange phase curve, its variational equation, the four-dimensional
normal variational equation, and a non-Abelian differential-Galois certificate
for every finite positive mass triple. Qualify that result before a new
`WP4-LIFT-NI-v3` consumes it.

In parallel, transform the qualified `WP1-CAT-N3BP-v1` catalogue into a
publication-gated residual-structure work map. That map may nominate only
local, partial, approximate, asymptotic, higher-variational, or non-meromorphic
targets. It may not report a new first integral.

residual map, and `WP4-LIFT-NI-v3`, plus clean-host parity. A failed frozen
