# WP3 v2 Exploration Boundary

This package performs dimensionless numerical screening only. A low singular
direction is not a first integral until a separate candidate qualification has
frozen and passed covariance, gauge, functional-independence, Poisson,
variational, long-time, sealed-holdout, and independent-replication tests.

The feature library is invariant under proper rotations. Scalar triple
products are intentionally retained because they distinguish the two parity
orientations that an O(3)-only Gram library identifies. The library is bounded
to products of at most two declared primitives and three symmetric mass
parameter blocks; a null result says nothing about functions outside that
library.

The known represented algebra generated by constants, total energy, squared
energy, and squared angular momentum is projected out before ranking. Numerical
value identities on the frozen corpus are removed independently by a weighted
value-space SVD.
