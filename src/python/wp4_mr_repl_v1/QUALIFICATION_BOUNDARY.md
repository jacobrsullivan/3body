# WP4-MR-REPL-v1 Qualification Boundary

A frozen PASS independently reproduces the four-dimensional Fuchsian normal
variational equation from the declared reduced Newtonian Hamiltonian and proves
that its differential-Galois identity component is non-Abelian for every
finite positive mass triple.

By the pinned Morales-Ramis theorem, this obstructs complete meromorphic
Liouville integrability of the declared planar reduced Hamiltonian in a complex
neighborhood of the fixed-nonzero-angular-momentum parabolic Lagrange phase
curve.

It does not say that the three-body problem has no additional integrals. It
does not address non-meromorphic regularity, zero angular momentum, the spatial
model without `WP4-LIFT-NI-v3`, arbitrary neighborhoods, orbit classification,
or triple collision. The project starts from the declared reduced Hamiltonian;
it does not claim to rederive every canonical reduction map from the
unreduced 18-dimensional state.
