Status: IN PROGRESS

## Immutable Baseline

`WP3-EXP-v1` remains unchanged. Its zero-nullity result is the baseline against
which the v2 search is evaluated. No v1 mode is promoted or reinterpreted.

## Qualification Tracks

1. `WP1Q-N3BP-SPATIAL-v1`: published Brutus spatial/chaotic states,
   arbitrary-precision checkpoints, and a separately sealed WP3 reserve.
2. `WP2-3BFLOWQ-v1`: clean-room reduction replication, canonical Jacobi flow,
   generic SO(3)-quotient flow and reconstruction, and physical-time constrained
   pairwise KS lift with gauge and closure dynamics.
3. `WP3-EXP-v2`: per-system nondimensionalization, full low-degree SO(3)
   invariant primitives including scalar triple products, parameter blocks,
   orbit-level weighting, known-integral algebra removal, and family-wise
   calibration.

## Staged Gates

- `SEARCH_READY`: external corpus, arbitrary-precision oracle, independent
  reduction, qualified reduced/lifted flow, and sealed publication reserve.
- `CANDIDATE_READY`: a numerical mode passes frozen residual, stability, and
  calibration gates without semantic interpretation.
- `PUBLICATION_READY`: the existing broad publication gate plus candidate
  covariance, independence, Poisson, variational, long-time, holdout, and
  independent replication evidence.

A no-candidate v2 result completes the campaign as a bounded null result. It
does not fabricate candidate-specific evidence or weaken the publication gate.
