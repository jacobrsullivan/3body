# WP1-RESIDUAL-MAP-v1 Plan

Date: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

Create a deterministic research work map from the already qualified
`WP1-CAT-N3BP-v1` catalogue without converting numerical patterns into
mathematical claims.

## Permitted Tracks

- `LOCAL_STABLE_NORMAL_FORM`: local normal-form, KAM nondegeneracy, and
  approximate-invariant tests around qualified planar-linearly-stable
  periodic orbits.
- `ASYMPTOTIC_MANIFOLD`: stable/unstable manifold, scattering, and
  homoclinic tests around qualified planar-linearly-unstable periodic orbits.
- `HIGHER_VARIATIONAL_EXCEPTION`: candidate-specific higher-variational
  analysis for an exact first-NVE exceptional mass value.
- `DOMAIN_RESTRICTED_OR_NONMEROMORPHIC`: explicitly restricted searches not
  contradicted by the meromorphic complete-integrability obstruction.

## Publication Gate

The output ranks evidence quality and assigns bounded follow-on protocols.
It does not discover, infer, name, or interpret an invariant. Every later
candidate claim requires a new, candidate-specific specification lock,
source-disjoint validation, negative controls, and a sealed holdout or exact
proof before interpretation.
