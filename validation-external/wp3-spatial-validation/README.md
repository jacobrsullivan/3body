# WP1Q-N3BP-SPATIAL-v1

This campaign qualifies a bounded genuinely spatial and chaotic extension of
the full Newtonian three-body evidence. Source rows come from the published
Brutus virialized Plummer supplements and retain their decimal initial and
terminal states, integration tolerance, and word length.

The qualification corpus and the separate WP3 publication reserve are selected
by one deterministic rule. Qualification rows are committed. Publication
reserve rows are represented only by HMAC commitments; their key and plaintext
remain outside the repository until a candidate-specific model and thresholds
are frozen.
