# WP4-LIFT-NI-v2 Qualification Boundary

This package checks exact algebraic obligations in the locked
`WP4-LIFT-NI-v2` transfer proof. It consumes the qualified
`WP2-MULTIQ-v1` model and the published planar normal-variational obstruction
already qualified by `WP4-MR-NI-v2`.

A passing campaign rules out a complete shell-regular physically admissible
meromorphic Liouville family on the qualified KS atlas. Physically admissible
means that the family includes `K`, `p_t`, `Xi1`, and `Xi2`, is pairwise
commuting, has ambient rank nine on a relative dense-open subset of
`J=0, K=0`, and its remaining five functions are gauge-invariant,
clock-independent, and compatible on all chart overlaps.

The result does not rule out ambient-only integrable families whose rank
disappears on the physical shell, or gauge-dependent, chart-dependent, or
explicitly clock-dependent extensions. It does not address non-meromorphic or
non-Liouville notions, nonzero-`Xi` magnetic dynamics, a general `SO(3)`
quotient, or triple-collision regularization. The package does not
independently rederive the published planar normal variational equation or its
differential Galois group.
