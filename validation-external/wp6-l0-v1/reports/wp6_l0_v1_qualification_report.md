## Authorized Claim

For arbitrary finite positive masses and every finite nonzero energy h, the planar Newtonian three-body problem restricted to the common level (H,C)=(h,0) is not rationally integrable in the locked restricted-integrability sense.

## Claim Boundary

A PASS qualifies failure of restricted rational complete integrability for the planar positive-mass three-body problem on every common level (H,C)=(h,0) with h nonzero. It does not cover (0,0), the spatial problem, non-rational regularity classes, absence of one additional integral, or a collision-regularized extension.

## Prohibited Claims

- The same witness proves nonintegrability on (H,C)=(0,0).
- No one additional integral exists on the zero-angular-momentum level.
- The spatial zero-angular-momentum problem is qualified by this track.
