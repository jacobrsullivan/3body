# WP3 Exploration Boundary

This package searches for near-null Lie-derivative modes in qualified 12D
canonical Jacobi variables. Its feature library is invariant under simultaneous
SO(3) rotation and includes energy and angular-momentum-squared controls.
The search is split into homogeneous mass-length-time dimension sectors;
features with different physical dimensions are never combined.

Every output is `EXPLORATORY_ONLY`. A small residual is a numerical screening
signal, not evidence of a new first integral. No candidate may be described as
conserved, independent, in involution, or publication-ready until the separate
publication gate passes.

The v1 exploration does not use the legacy WP3 lifted metrics, mechanical
connection, synthetic HNN demonstrations, or placeholder pairwise dynamics. It
does not claim a qualified reduced/lifted N3BP vector field or hidden
integrability.

The v1 feature basis and universal-coefficient ansatz are finite. Failure to
find an additional numerical-null direction is not evidence that no additional
first integral exists outside that ansatz.
